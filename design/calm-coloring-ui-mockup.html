Calm Coloring

A working click-through of the two v1 screens — template gallery and the tap-to-fill canvas — built to the calm-design constraints in §5 of the PRD. Tap a template, then tap a shape to watch the fill settle in. This stands in for the visual design step; nothing here is final until it's reviewed.

Calm-design rules applied

  • SoundNone. No fill chime, no ambient music.
  • AnimationOnly the slow color-settle on tap — no other motion anywhere.
  • RewardNo confetti, badges, or "you did it!" popups.
  • BrightnessOne small muted palette, reused for chrome and fill alike.

System palette

Sage Sky Clay Sand Lilac Moss

Six muted swatches total, per §5.2 — the same set tints the gallery cards and fills the coloring regions. No separate "bright" child palette.

8 of the 15 launch templates are built out here as real, tappable scenes so the interaction and palette can be judged properly; the remaining launch templates follow the same construction once art direction is signed off. Print exports from the same vector paths shown on screen (§5.4) — the print button here is a placeholder for that flow.
Share is new — a parent-facing action next to Print that watermarks the finished piece with "Painted by {name}" and hands it to WhatsApp, Instagram, or the system share sheet ("More"). The name is typed fresh each time here and never leaves the device; open question for planning — should this sit behind a simple parental gate, consistent with how App/Play Store kids policies usually treat any outbound share from a children's app.

Reference point: Pok Pok's calm, Montessori-inspired visual language — no overstimulating palette, no menu chrome — informed the restraint here without borrowing its assets.
