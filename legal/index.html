Privacy Policy

Calm Coloring is a tap-to-fill coloring app for young children. It is built, deliberately, to need as little of your data as possible. This page explains exactly what that means in practice.

Data controller

Calm Coloring is developed by an independent developer, Atul (contact details under Contact below), who is the data controller for the purposes of this policy and, where applicable, the GDPR.

What we collect

Nothing personal, and no accounts. Calm Coloring does not require sign-up, does not create user accounts or profiles, and does not collect names, email addresses, advertising identifiers, or any other personal information from you or your child.

What the app does on your device

  • Coloring & templates — all coloring happens locally on the device. Nothing about how a child colors a page is recorded, transmitted, or stored anywhere outside the device.
  • Content updates — the app periodically fetches new coloring-page artwork (line-art shapes only — no personal data) over a secure (HTTPS) connection, from GitHub's content-delivery infrastructure (raw.githubusercontent.com), so new templates can be added without an app update. This is a one-way download of static content; the app itself sends no information about you. As with any network request, your device's IP address is necessarily visible to — and may be briefly logged by — GitHub as the hosting provider, purely as an ordinary function of delivering the file; we do not receive, access, or otherwise process that log data ourselves.
  • Printing — printing a picture uses your device's own print system and never leaves the device.
  • Sharing — if a grown-up chooses to share a finished picture (to WhatsApp, Instagram, or any other app via the system share sheet), only the rendered picture and an optional caption (the artist's name, if typed in) are sent — and only to the destination the grown-up picks. That name is drawn directly onto the shared image; Calm Coloring never stores it or sends it anywhere else. Sharing is always a deliberate, user-initiated action, and it sits behind a simple "grown-up check" (a basic math question) so a young child can't trigger it by themselves.

Advertising, analytics & third-party SDKs

Calm Coloring contains no ad SDKs, no analytics/tracking SDKs, and no third-party data-collection libraries of any kind. There are no ads in the app.

Children's privacy

Calm Coloring is designed to be safe for young children, in the spirit of COPPA and similar children's-privacy laws: it collects no personal information from anyone, child or adult, so there is nothing to disclose or delete on a child's behalf. If you believe this app has somehow collected information it shouldn't have, please contact us using the details below and we will investigate and remove it.

Data retention & deletion

Because no personal data is collected or stored on our servers, there is no account or personal data to request deletion of. Locally cached template artwork can be cleared at any time by clearing the app's storage in your device settings, or by uninstalling the app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same address with a new "last updated" date above.

Additional information for EU/EEA & UK users (GDPR)

This section is written to satisfy the GDPR's transparency requirements directly, on top of the plain-language summary above.

Legal basis for processing

Calm Coloring does not collect or process any personal data of its own, so in the ordinary sense there is no processing activity requiring a legal basis under Article 6 GDPR. To the limited extent any processing occurs at all — the incidental logging of your IP address by our hosting providers (GitHub Pages / raw.githubusercontent.com) as a routine, unavoidable part of serving any web request — the legal basis is our legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f)) in operating the app's content-delivery infrastructure securely and reliably. This data is not used for any other purpose, such as profiling or advertising.

International data transfers

Our static content and this policy are hosted on GitHub Pages / GitHub's CDN, operated by GitHub, Inc. (a Microsoft subsidiary) in the United States. Microsoft is a certified participant in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (and its UK/Swiss extensions), which is the safeguard relied on for this incidental, minimal transfer. No other data leaves the EU/EEA through use of this app.

Data retention

We hold no personal data of our own, so we have no retention period to disclose for anything under our control. Any incidental server-log entries generated by our hosting provider are retained and eventually rotated according to that provider's own standard operational practices, not ours.

Your rights under the GDPR

Because Calm Coloring collects no personal data, most of these rights will already be satisfied by default (there is nothing held about you to access, correct, or delete). Nonetheless, if you believe we hold any information about you, you have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of any personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectification — request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Erasure — request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Restriction — request that we limit how we use your data.
  • Portability — request your data in a portable format.
  • Objection — object to processing based on legitimate interest.
  • Lodge a complaint — with your local data protection supervisory authority, at any time, in addition to (not instead of) contacting us.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Contact below.

Children & GDPR Article 8

Article 8 GDPR requires parental consent before relying on a child's own consent as the legal basis for processing their personal data. Calm Coloring sidesteps this requirement entirely by design — it processes no personal data from children (or anyone) in the first place, so no consent of any kind is collected from, or required of, a child.
